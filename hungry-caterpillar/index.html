01 · Teams or players

Click a monster to change it. If you don't type a name, the team takes the monster's name. With 1 team the whole class plays together and tries to beat the record.

02 · How it works

The caterpillar starts very small, with one letter. The first team says a word that starts with that letter (W → wolf). The caterpillar eats it and grows. The next team needs a word that starts with the last letter (F → family), and so on.
Points = letters in the word (wolf = 4 points). The game ends when a team gives a word that is wrong or was already used: the caterpillar gets sick and… 💩
