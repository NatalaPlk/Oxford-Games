03 · Teams & monsters

Click a monster to change it. If you don't type a name, the team takes the monster's name.

04 · Game options

In each round every team has one turn. The team looks at the picture and clicks the right word out of three similar words (same unit, same kind of word). Right = the monster jumps forward one square · Wrong = the monster is sad and the right word lights up green. A turn ends when the pictures run out or the time is up.
